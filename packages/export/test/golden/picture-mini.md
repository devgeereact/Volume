# Today's New Word

**Brave**

Being brave means trying something even when you feel a little bit wobbly inside.

# Spread 1

At the bottom of the garden, under the old apple tree, lived a small grey mouse called Mabel. She had soft round ears and a tail like a curl of string.

# Spread 2

Every evening Mabel watched the moon rise over the garden wall. "One day," she whispered, "I will see the moonlit garden up close."

# Spread 3

But the garden looked very big at night, and Mabel felt a little bit wobbly inside. Her friend Hedge the hedgehog came snuffling by.

# Spread 4

"Being brave means trying, even when you feel wobbly," said Hedge kindly. "Shall we go together?" Mabel took a deep breath and nodded.

# Spread 5

Step by step, paw by paw, they crept along the garden path. The stones were cool and smooth, and the grass smelled sweet.

# Spread 6

Pop, pop, pop! Tiny lights blinked on all around them. "Glow-worms!" laughed Hedge. Mabel giggled. Being brave felt warm and fizzy.

# Spread 7

By the pond, a frog sang a slow, sleepy song. The water shone like a silver plate, and the moon smiled down at them.

# Spread 8

Mabel climbed the tallest flowerpot all by herself. From the top she could see the whole garden, soft and silver in the moonlight.

# Spread 9

"I was brave!" said Mabel proudly. "You were," said Hedge, "and I was brave too, because we were brave together."

# Spread 10

Back under the apple tree, Mabel snuggled into her mossy bed. The moon shone gently through the leaves. Goodnight, brave little Mabel.
