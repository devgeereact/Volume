<section epub:type="chapter" class="chapter" id="chapter-1-start-where-you-are"><header><p class="kicker">Chapter 1</p><h1>Start Where You Are</h1></header><p class="noindent">Before you change anything, look at what you already do.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-1-start-where-you-are-s1">Notice Your Week</h2>
<p class="noindent">Most homes have one day that goes wrong. For me it was Sunday evening, when the week ahead arrived all at once.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-1-start-where-you-are-s3">Pick One Small Corner</h2>
<p class="noindent">A whole house is too much to think about. A shelf, a drawer or the hall table is not.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-1-start-where-you-are-s5">Keep It Short</h2>
<p class="noindent">Ten minutes is enough. Stop when the timer goes, even if you are halfway through.</p>
<aside class="exercise"><p class="xh">Exercise: Your Busiest Day</p><p class="noindent">Which day of the week feels most crowded at home, and why?</p><p class="write-space">Space to write your answer.</p></aside>
<aside class="exercise"><p class="xh">Exercise: Before You Start</p><ul class="checklist"><li>Choose one corner to reset first</li><li>Set a ten-minute timer on your phone</li><li>Put a bag for things to give away by the door</li></ul></aside>
<aside class="takeaways"><p class="th">Key Takeaways</p><ul><li>Look at your week before you change it.</li><li>Start with one small corner, not the whole house.</li><li>Ten minutes, then stop.</li></ul></aside></section>

<section epub:type="chapter" class="chapter" id="chapter-2-make-it-a-habit"><header><p class="kicker">Chapter 2</p><h1>Make It a Habit</h1></header><p class="noindent">A reset only helps if it happens again next week.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-2-make-it-a-habit-s1">Choose a Time</h2>
<p class="noindent">Tie the reset to something you already do, such as the moment the kettle goes on after dinner.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-2-make-it-a-habit-s3">Track It</h2>
<p class="noindent">Ticking a box is a small reward. It also shows you the weeks that slipped.</p>
<h2 id="chapter-2-make-it-a-habit-s5">Plan for Bad Weeks</h2>
<p class="noindent">Some weeks will go wrong. Decide now what the smallest possible reset looks like.</p>

<aside class="takeaways"><p class="th">Key Takeaways</p><ul><li>Tie the reset to a habit you already have.</li><li>Tick the log so you can see the pattern.</li><li>Plan the smallest reset for bad weeks.</li></ul></aside>
