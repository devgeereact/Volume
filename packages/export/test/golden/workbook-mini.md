# How to Use This Workbook

Read one chapter a week. Each chapter ends with exercises: write in the lines, tick the boxes and fill in the blanks. There are no wrong answers.

# Introduction

I wrote this workbook for people whose homes feel busy even when nothing is happening. You do not need a free weekend. You need ten minutes and a pen.

# Chapter 1: Start Where You Are

Before you change anything, look at what you already do.

## Notice Your Week

Most homes have one day that goes wrong. For me it was Sunday evening, when the week ahead arrived all at once.

## Pick One Small Corner

A whole house is too much to think about. A shelf, a drawer or the hall table is not.

## Keep It Short

Ten minutes is enough. Stop when the timer goes, even if you are halfway through.

::: exercise reflection Your Busiest Day

# Chapter 2: Make It a Habit

A reset only helps if it happens again next week.

## Choose a Time

Tie the reset to something you already do, such as the moment the kettle goes on after dinner.

## Track It

Ticking a box is a small reward. It also shows you the weeks that slipped.

## Plan for Bad Weeks
