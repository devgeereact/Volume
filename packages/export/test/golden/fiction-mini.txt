CHAPTER 1: THE LIGHT COMES BACK

The lamp at Gull Point had been dark for eleven years when Morwenna Tregear saw
it burning from her kitchen window.

She set down the kettle and watched. The beam swung once across the bay, slow
and yellow, and then it was gone.

"You saw it too," said her brother from the doorway. He had his coat on already.

* * *

The path to the point was slick with rain. Morwenna went first, torch in one
hand, the other on the cold iron rail her father had fixed to the rock.

At the top the door of the lamp room stood open. Someone had swept the floor.

CHAPTER 2: FOOTPRINTS IN THE SALT

By morning the whole village had an opinion. Mrs Kitto at the shop said it was
boys from Penzance. The vicar said nothing at all, which Morwenna noticed.

She went back up alone after lunch. The floor was still clean, but a line of
salty footprints led from the stairs to the window and stopped.

* * *

Her brother was waiting at the bottom of the path when she came down.

"Well?" he said.

"Whoever it was," she said, "they never came back down."
