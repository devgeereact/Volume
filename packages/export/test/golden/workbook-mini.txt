HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK

Read one chapter a week. Each chapter ends with exercises: write in the lines,
tick the boxes and fill in the blanks. There are no wrong answers.

================================================================================

INTRODUCTION

I wrote this workbook for people whose homes feel busy even when nothing is
happening. You do not need a free weekend. You need ten minutes and a pen.

================================================================================

CHAPTER 1: START WHERE YOU ARE

Before you change anything, look at what you already do.

Notice Your Week

Most homes have one day that goes wrong. For me it was Sunday evening, when the
week ahead arrived all at once.

Pick One Small Corner

A whole house is too much to think about. A shelf, a drawer or the hall table is
not.

Keep It Short

Ten minutes is enough. Stop when the timer goes, even if you are halfway
through.

EXERCISE: YOUR BUSIEST DAY

Which day of the week feels most crowded at home, and why?

Space to write your answer.

EXERCISE: BEFORE YOU START

[ ] Choose one corner to reset first
[ ] Set a ten-minute timer on your phone
[ ] Put a bag for things to give away by the door

KEY TAKEAWAYS

- Look at your week before you change it.
- Start with one small corner, not the whole house.
- Ten minutes, then stop.

================================================================================

CHAPTER 2: MAKE IT A HABIT

A reset only helps if it happens again next week.

Choose a Time

Tie the reset to something you already do, such as the moment the kettle goes on
after dinner.

Track It

Ticking a box is a small reward. It also shows you the weeks that slipped.

Plan for Bad Weeks

Some weeks will go wrong. Decide now what the smallest possible reset looks
like.

EXERCISE: YOUR RESET PLAN

EXERCISE: A BUSY SATURDAY

Sam planned a reset for Saturday morning, but friends arrived early and the day
filled up.

- What could Sam do instead of skipping the week?
- What would you do in the same place?

Space to write your answer.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

- Tie the reset to a habit you already have.
- Tick the log so you can see the pattern.
- Plan the smallest reset for bad weeks.

CONCLUSION

You now have a plan, a log and a fallback for bad weeks. Keep this book near the
corner you started with.
