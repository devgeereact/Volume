# Prompts

Every prompt Volume sends to a model lives in `prompts/` as a versioned Markdown file. This page indexes them. The file format, variables and versioning rules are in `prompts/README.md`; the models each stage uses are in `config/models.defaults.json`.

39 prompts. Version 1 of each was written on 4 October 2026 for fully automatic mode: the AI writes the whole book in the first person from the author profile, inside the house style (`config/house-style.json`) and the honesty guardrails. The version 2 files (5 October 2026) put the banned list in its own block. The round 5 versions (5 October 2026, after a live book) add the profile facts block (`{{profile.facts}}`) with a hard rule never to state personal facts, relationships, history or credentials the profile does not hold, the grouped used-already list, word targets for the introduction and conclusion, the book word target and book-type structure in the blueprint, personal claims in `checks.claims`, warnings in `checks.summarise-and-ledger`, the cover concept count, and the new `writing.extend-section`. The round 6 versions (5 October 2026, after live run 3) stop the writing prompts inventing the author's past experience or conversations, state the author's background at most once in the body (`{{profile.factsUsedIn}}`), never name a firm as a scam or regulated without a research source, add dated prices (`{{book.datedPrices}}`, `{{book.checkedMonth}}`) and let pages promise dated prices only when the app's check passed (`{{book.pricesCheck}}`); they also tighten resources and the glossary, judge each section against its own plan and catch references to scenes never written (`checks.consistency`), skip composite-story lines and add named-firm claims (`checks.claims`), add page titles to research source notes, add scaffolding openers to the style rewrite, and keep marketing to what the written chapters teach. The clean draft versions (21 October 2026: `writing.chapter`, `writing.introduction`, `writing.conclusion` and `writing.back-matter` v7, `writing.extend-section` v5, `checks.summarise-and-ledger` v6) add the book type's must-haves and things to avoid as plain text (`{{bookTypeRules}}`), a clean draft rules block (filled-in examples, no invented brands or case-study people, no guarantees, no unsourced statistics, the humanizer rules) and short summaries for front and back matter pages. Older versions stay in place for books pinned to them.

## Pipeline order

1. Research: `research.title-research` (web search; sources only from `url_citation` annotations).
2. Titles: `titles.topic-directions`, then `titles.title-options`.
3. Blueprint: `blueprint.blueprint`; any field again with `blueprint.regenerate-part`.
4. Writing, per chapter: `writing.chapter` (and one `writing.extend-section` pass when a section comes back under 85% of its word target), then `checks.summarise-and-ledger` (summary and used-already ledger for the next chapter), then code checks and `checks.consistency` and `checks.claims`. Front and back matter: `writing.introduction`, `writing.conclusion`, `writing.front-matter`, `writing.back-matter`. Editor: `writing.rewrite-selection`, `writing.weave-note` and `writing.expand-note` (author notes woven in, or expanded into a new subsection).
5. Editing: `editing.structural`, `editing.line-edit-uk`, `editing.voice`. Claims to verify: `editing.claim-fix` (three replacement options for one flagged sentence, on the owner's request).
6. Matter and metadata: `matter.amazon-description`, `matter.keywords-categories`, `matter.back-cover`.
7. Covers: `cover.cover-brief`, then `cover.cover-image` writes the text-free prompt the image model receives.
8. Marketing: `marketing.tiktok-scripts`, `marketing.instagram-captions`, `marketing.launch-email`.
9. Fiction and workbooks (phase 6): the app picks the prompt by book template with `taskForTemplate` in `packages/ai`. Fiction books run `blueprint.blueprint-fiction` (with the story bible, D-043), `blueprint.regenerate-part-fiction`, `writing.chapter-fiction` (scene breaks as `***`, no subheadings or takeaways) and `writing.extend-section-fiction` (deepens scenes, never adds a heading); the story bible is sent once, as `{{storyBible}}`, and left out of `{{blueprint}}`; workbooks run `blueprint.blueprint-workbook` (2 to 4 planned exercises per chapter) and `writing.chapter-workbook` (the block dialect of D-042). Every other template keeps `blueprint.blueprint` and `writing.chapter`. `checks.summarise-and-ledger` v5 logs fiction characters and settings, and `checks.consistency` v4 checks fiction against the story bible and workbook exercise blocks.
10. Picture books (phase 7): `children.picture-book-story`, then `children.scene-prompts`, then `children.character-reference`. The app paints one reference image per main character, then one 2:1 image per spread with those references and a fixed seed where the model takes one (D-045).

## Stages that share a model

- `checks.summarise-and-ledger` runs on the `summarise` stage model (small), not the `checks` model, so the owner can tune them apart.
- `cover.cover-image`, `children.scene-prompts` and `children.character-reference` run on the `cover_brief` text model. Their `imagePrompt` output is what the `cover_image` stage (the image model, `POST /api/v1/images`) receives.
- `writing.rewrite-selection` runs on the `editing` model from v5 (18 October 2026), as the server's editing job stage already did; v4 and earlier say `writing`. `writing.weave-note` and `writing.expand-note` run on the `writing` model (the server's `weave` job stage maps to it).
- `editing.claim-fix` runs on the `editing` model; the server's `claim_fix` job stage maps to it.
- `children.picture-book-story`, `writing.chapter-fiction` and `writing.chapter-workbook` run on the `writing` model; `blueprint.blueprint-fiction` and `blueprint.blueprint-workbook` on the `blueprint` model.

## What changed from the drafts

- No ISBN is ever generated (the drafts asked for a "realistic ISBN"). The ISBN appears only if the owner entered one.
- The copyright year is filled automatically (the drafts fixed it at 2025).
- The introduction no longer includes testimonials (step 7 of the drafts' introduction formula): the author's own reason for writing replaces it.
- "Readers never suspect AI" is dropped: the prose is natural, and the KDP AI disclosure is set to AI-generated (KDP fact 5.5).
- Quote and "did you know" hooks use only quotes the owner supplied or facts from cited research sources; otherwise the chapter opens with a scene.
- Channel-specific calls to action in the children's drafts ("follow our page") are now an optional `{{cta}}` the owner fills.
- About the author is never written by AI: it is the profile bio word for word.

## How to add or change a prompt

1. Never edit a released version. Copy `prompts/<folder>/<name>.v<N>.md` to `.v<N+1>.md`.
2. Set `version` in the front matter, change the text, and add a `# Changes` section at the bottom saying what changed and why.
3. Keep the front matter complete: `id`, `version`, `stage`, `tier`, `inputs` (every `{{variable}}` the body uses), `output`, `derived_from`, `guardrails`.
4. KDP numbers only through `{{kdp.*}}` variables; banned phrases only through `{{houseStyle.bannedPhrases}}`. If a prompt must quote banned words on purpose, wrap them in `<!-- house-style:off -->` and `<!-- house-style:on -->`.
5. A new prompt also needs: its id in the stage's `prompts` list in `config/models.defaults.json`, a mock fixture in `packages/ai/test/fixtures/`, and a row in the table above.
6. Check before you hand over: the front matter parses as YAML, every variable in the body is in `inputs`, and the file has no em dashes and no banned phrases outside the marked blocks.
7. Add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`. Books pinned to an older version keep using it.

## Honesty revision, 19 October 2026

After the live acceptance run of 18 October 2026, where a nonfiction book stamped 16 invented prices "(checked October 2026)", narrated scenes such as "The author set a personal ceiling of £8 a month" and shipped a blurb that described its own language and tone instead of selling the book, these versions change:

- `writing.chapter` v6, `writing.chapter-workbook` v2, `writing.introduction` v6, `writing.extend-section` v4 and `writing.back-matter` v6: a price, fee, rate, statistic or "checked" date only when a research source states it, with its `[[S<n>]]` marker; the "(checked <month year>)" stamp goes only on a sourced amount. Without a source the text points the reader to the current price, uses a clearly hypothetical "you" figure with no stamp, or leaves the figure out. The author's own experiences come only from the profile facts, in the first or the third person, and "the author" is never a character in a scene. Composites are about other people, never the author's own life.
- `writing.conclusion` v6 and `writing.front-matter` v6: these parts have no research sources, so they state no real prices and write no "checked" stamps; the same rule on the author's experiences.
- `matter.back-cover` v7 and `matter.amazon-description` v6: the blurb and description sell the book to the reader (the problem, the promise, who it is for and three to five concrete things inside) and never describe the text itself (language, tone, style, composites). Still 120 to 200 words for the blurb, with no reviews, rankings or awards.
- `blueprint.blueprint` v6 and `blueprint.blueprint-workbook` v2: story ideas never make the author a character, and the blurb and description drafts sell the book.
- Checked and left as they are: `writing.chapter-fiction`, `writing.extend-section-fiction`, `blueprint.blueprint-fiction` and `children.picture-book-story` already forbid statistics, sources and real-world advice, and invented people and scenes are the work in fiction.

The guardrails in `packages/ai` back this up with two code checks that send hits to the claims report: `checked-stamp-unsourced` (a "(checked <Month> <Year>)" stamp with no research source marker in its sentence) and `author-anecdote` (the author narrated as a character, or a first-person past experience the profile facts do not hold). Older versions stay in place for books pinned to them.
