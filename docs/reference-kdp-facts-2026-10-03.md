# P02 Fact Check: AI Book Writing and Amazon KDP Toolkit

Checked on 3 October 2026 against official pages only. Every fact below was read on 3 October 2026.

Status key:

- **CONFIRMED**: seen on the official page named.
- **PARTIAL**: official page found, but the exact detail was not shown, was only in a search summary, or was inconsistent.
- **NOT FOUND**: no official page stating it was found. Do not print it as fact.

Method note: pages were read through an automated fetch that summarises each page. Short quotes below are what that fetch returned from the page. Prices and limits change, so every number in the guide should carry a "check before you rely on this" line.

## 1. KDP account and tax

**1.1** You create a KDP account at kdp.amazon.com either by signing in with an existing Amazon account or by choosing "Create your KDP account" and entering a name, email and password. Before publishing you complete account details (individual or company, address), a bank account under "Getting Paid", and the tax information.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200620010 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.2** KDP must receive and validate your tax identity before you can publish new books or update existing ones. Quote: "We must receive and validate your tax identity before you can update existing books or publish new books."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274750 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.3** The tax interview produces Form W-8BEN for "Non-U.S. individuals claiming foreign status and/or treaty benefits", which is the form a UK individual author would complete.
Source: https://account.kdp.amazon.com/tax-interview/help?nodeId=201600460&locale=en_US (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.4** By default, royalties on eBook sales on Amazon.com, print sales on Amazon.com, .co.jp, .com.au and .ca, and KDP Select Global Fund payments are subject to 30% US withholding, which may be reduced if your country has a tax treaty with the US. If you have no US TIN you "may enter the income tax identification number issued to you by the tax authority in your country of residence."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274690 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.5** The UK treaty rate on US-source copyright royalties is 0% (IRS Table 1, Rev. May 2023, United Kingdom row, "Copyrights" column 12 = 0, treaty article 12(1)). KDP's own pages do not name the UK rate; they only point to treaty rates generally.
Source: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-lbi/tax-treaty-table-1.pdf (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED** on the IRS table. KDP pages do not state the UK figure, so the guide should say "may be reduced to 0% under the UK and US treaty if you qualify" rather than promise it.

**1.6** A W-8 form "will expire on the last day of the third succeeding calendar year from the date signed", so the tax interview must be redone periodically.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201274690 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.7** UK bank accounts can be paid by direct deposit (EFT), listed for UK accounts in GBP/EUR, with no minimum payment threshold. KDP stopped offering cheque payments in the UK for new accounts in October 2017.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GJD7DFFX6X88AK46 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**1.8** Royalties are paid monthly, "approximately 60 days after the end of the month in which the sale was reported (90 days for Expanded Distribution)." Direct deposit usually arrives in 1 to 5 business days after payment is issued.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GK2MKZUL6U3SFBPZ (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## 2. eBook royalties

**2.1** KDP offers two eBook royalty options: 35% of the list price without VAT, or 70% of the list price without VAT less delivery costs (70% applies only to sales in eligible territories; 35% applies elsewhere). Public domain works can only use 35%.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.2** For amazon.co.uk, the 70% option requires a list price between £1.77 and £12.99. The EU/UK price page labels this the "minimum and maximum list price with VAT" and dates the range "as of July 7, 2026".
Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560 and https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201645450 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.3** For amazon.co.uk at 35%, the price range depends on file size: under 3 MB £0.77 to £150.00; 3 MB to under 10 MB £1.25 to £150.00; 10 MB or more £1.49 to £150.00.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.4** The delivery cost deducted on 70% sales for amazon.co.uk is £0.10 per MB. Quote: "Amazon.co.uk: UK £0.10/MB".
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.5** The UK VAT rate on eBooks has been 0% since 1 May 2020, and KDP calculates royalties on the price after deducting any applicable VAT.
Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634500 and https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. Note: KDP's pages describe EU/UK eBook list prices as VAT-inclusive; with UK eBook VAT at 0% the effect on a UK price is nil. The guide should not go further than that.

**2.6** KDP Select is "a free 90-day program for Kindle eBook only"; enrolled eBooks "must also be exclusive to the Kindle Store for the KDP Select enrollment period" and are "automatically renewed unless you opt out". Print and audio can still be sold elsewhere.
Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200798990 and https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GD9PMU58BV24QFZ7 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.7** Enrolled eBooks are automatically included in Kindle Unlimited. KU royalties are a share of the monthly KDP Select Global Fund based on pages read, measured in Kindle Edition Normalized Pages (KENP), capped at "3,000 Kindle Edition Normalized Pages (KENP) Read per title per customer."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201541130 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**2.8** KDP Select benefits include Free Book Promotions and Kindle Countdown Deals, and "Kindle Countdown Deals can only be run on the Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk marketplaces."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200798990 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## 3. Paperback (and hardcover)

**3.1** Paperback royalties on Amazon marketplaces are now tiered, not a flat 60%. On amazon.co.uk the rate is 50% for list prices at or below £7.98 and 60% at or above £7.99. Formula: "(Royalty rate x list price) – printing costs = royalty".
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. The page does not give the date this tiering started.

**3.2** Expanded Distribution pays 40% of the list price minus printing costs.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834330 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.3** amazon.co.uk black-ink printing cost, regular trim: 24 to 110 pages costs a flat £1.93; 110 to 828 pages costs £0.85 fixed plus £0.010 per page. Large trim: £2.15 flat at the low end, then £0.85 plus £0.012 per page. The page's UK worked example is a 55-page black-ink book costing £1.93.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED** (the page range boundary was returned as "24 - 110" and "110 - 828", which overlap at 110; check the live table before quoting the boundary). Worked example for a 200-page 6 x 9 in black-ink book: £0.85 + (200 x £0.010) = £2.85. This sum is ours, not KDP's.

**3.4** The minimum paperback list price is the printing cost divided by the royalty rate (for the 60% tier, printing cost / 0.6), so that "your royalties earned are always enough to cover the cost to print your book."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834340 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.5** For print books on amazon.co.uk you set a VAT-exclusive list price and Amazon adds the applicable VAT for customers. Most UK books are at the 0% rate, but some (for example notebooks, planners, and books with bonus content) are at 20%. "Your royalties are not impacted by VAT rate variations."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GPQL5W3J6WNRCZTV (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.6** 6 x 9 in is "the most common trim size for paperbacks in the U.S."; a 6 x 9 in black-ink, white-paper paperback can be 24 to 828 pages. Across options, paperback maximums range from 590 to 828 pages; the minimum is 24.
Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6 and https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.7** Bleed: add 0.125 in to the width (outside edge) and 0.25 in to the height (top and bottom), so a 6 x 9 in book with bleed is set up at 6.125 x 9.25 in.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.8** The paperback cover must be a single PDF containing back cover, spine and front cover, with 0.125 in (3.2 mm) bleed on all outer edges, images at least 300 DPI. KDP's cover calculator and template generator takes binding, ink, paper, trim, units and page count, and gives exact dimensions plus a PDF/PNG template.
Sources: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020 and https://kdp.amazon.com/cover-calculator (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.9** Spine width = page count x 0.002252 in for white paper, or x 0.0025 in for cream paper.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**3.10** Spine text needs at least 79 pages ("To include spine text, your book must have at least 79 pages"); with Cover Creator the minimum is 80. Covers with spine text on books under 79 pages are rejected.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**, with a caution: the same page also says "We only print spine text on books with more than 79 pages." Tell readers to use 80 pages or more to be safe.

**3.11** Hardcovers are available on KDP, including on amazon.co.uk, with 75 to 550 pages, five trim sizes, and 50% and 60% royalty rates.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GAVW3FZZAKA2KY3B (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## 4. ISBN and legal deposit

**4.1** KDP offers a free ISBN for paperback and hardcover only. "Free ISBNs from us can only be used on KDP. They cannot be used to publish outside of KDP." The imprint then shows as "Independently published".
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.2** An ISBN is optional for Kindle eBooks on KDP.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834170 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.3** Low-content books are not eligible for the free KDP ISBN.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GTJ8LBXL6Z4WV5QX (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **PARTIAL**. Seen only in a search summary of this KDP page; the page itself was not opened.

**4.4** Nielsen UK ISBN Store prices, VAT included: 1 ISBN £93.00; 10 ISBNs £174.00; 100 ISBNs £387.00; 1,000 ISBNs £994.00. Buyers "must be based in the UK, Ireland or a British Overseas Territory."
Source: https://www.nielsenisbnstore.com/Home/ISBN (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.5** "If you publish anything in the UK and Ireland, you need to give one copy to the British Library." Legal deposit "applies to any publication distributed in the UK and Ireland, regardless of content, format or language." Address: Legal Deposit Office, The British Library, Boston Spa, Wetherby, West Yorkshire, LS23 7BY.
Source: https://www.bl.uk/services/legal-deposit (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.6** The British Library copy must be delivered "within one month beginning with the day of publication."
Source: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/28/section/4 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED** in the Act. The BL services page did not show the deadline in what we could read.

**4.7** The five other legal deposit libraries (National Library of Scotland, National Library of Wales, Bodleian, Cambridge University Library, Trinity College Dublin) may request a copy, through the Agency for Legal Deposit Libraries, within 12 months of publication; the copy is then due within one month of the request.
Sources: https://www.bl.uk/services/legal-deposit and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/28/section/5 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.8** Self-publishing authors are covered: "all publishers (including self-publishing authors and composers) are obliged to provide one copy of each of their published works, free of charge, to the British Library." (BL blog post dated 1 October 2025, written about music scores but stating the general rule.)
Source: https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/call-for-composers-and-publishers-to-deposit-digital-scores (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**4.9** Whether a print-on-demand book printed outside the UK (for example by KDP) triggers the deposit duty: no British Library statement found. The Act's duty falls on "A person who publishes in the United Kingdom" a work (s.1), and "publication" means "the issue of copies of the work to the public" (s.14). We did not find official guidance applying this to overseas POD printing.
Sources: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/28/section/1 and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2003/28/section/14 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **NOT FOUND**. The guide should not claim POD books are exempt or included; it should tell UK self-publishers to send a copy or ask the BL Legal Deposit Office.

**4.10** The BL's legal deposit service has been disrupted since the October 2023 cyber-attack; the page says "please continue to submit your print publications", while electronic submission is unavailable.
Source: https://www.bl.uk/services/legal-deposit (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## 5. Metadata and content rules

**5.1** You can enter up to seven keywords or short phrases. Avoid things such as quality claims, time-sensitive words ("new", "on sale"), unauthorised brand names, and programme names like "Kindle Unlimited". No exact character limit is stated on the page.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201298500 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.2** "You can select 3 categories when creating a book in KDP."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652170 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.3** The book description limit is 4,000 characters, and HTML tags count towards it.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201189630 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.4** "Your title and subtitle together must be fewer than 200 characters."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.5** AI-generated means the AI tool created the actual content, "even if you applied substantial edits afterwards". AI-assisted means you created the content yourself and used AI tools "to edit, refine, error-check, or otherwise improve" it. You must tell KDP about AI-generated content (text, images or translations) when publishing or republishing; for AI-assisted use, "It is not necessary to inform us."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.6** KDP does not allow content "that's typically disappointing to customers", descriptive content "meant to mislead customers", or "undifferentiated versions of public domain titles if a free version is available in our store."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**5.7** KDP limits title creation: "we limit the number of titles you can create at the same time to 2 per book format each week."
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202172740 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. The older "3 titles per day" figure is out of date. The reset time (reported in search results as Sunday 00:00 UTC) and start date were not visible on an official page we could open: **PARTIAL** for those two details.

## 6. File formats and tools

**6.1** Recommended eBook manuscript formats are Word (DOC/DOCX), Kindle Package Format (KPF, from Kindle Create) and EPUB. Also accepted: HTML, RTF, TXT and PDF (PDF for certain languages only). MOBI is no longer accepted for fixed-layout eBooks from March 2025.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634390 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**6.2** Paperback manuscripts can be uploaded as PDF, DOC, DOCX, RTF, HTML or TXT; PDF is required where images or elements run to the page edge (bleed). KDP converts other formats to PDF.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201857950 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**6.3** Kindle Create runs on Windows 10 or later and on Mac (Intel macOS 10.15 or later, Apple silicon macOS 11 or later), imports DOC/DOCX (and PDF for some book types), exports KPF (or EPUB for reflowable books), and a reflowable Kindle Create file can also be used for a print book.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GUGQ4WDZ92F733GC (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**6.4** Kindle Create is free.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200735480 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **PARTIAL**. The getting-started page does not say "free". The tools page returned "free desktop standalone application" for both Kindle Create and Kindle Previewer in identical wording, which may be a summarising error. The Amazon Kindle Create landing page returned an error (HTTP 503).

**6.5** Kindle Previewer is "a free desktop standalone application" for Windows 10 (64-bit) and above and Mac (macOS 12 and above). It previews Fire tablets, Kindle for Android, Kindle for iOS and Kindle e-readers, and opens .epub, .kpf, .doc, .docx, .htm/.html, .xhtml and .opf files.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202131170 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**6.6** KDP provides Word manuscript templates for paperback and hardcover, either blank (page size and margins set) or with sample content, by trim size. KDP advises saving the finished file as PDF with fonts embedded and removing all placeholder text.
Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834230 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**6.7** Reedsy Studio's "core writing and formatting features are completely free"; it exports EPUB "with one click" and typesets a "print-ready PDF". Paid add-ons: Studio Essential $4.99/month and Outlining $7.99/month, with a 30-day free trial.
Source: https://reedsy.com/studio (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. Word import: the page says you can import "from any major text format" but does not name .docx: **PARTIAL**.

Source: https://draft2digital.com/faq/ (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. This contradicts the common "free, only takes a cut" description, so update any copy that says D2D is free.

Sources: https://authors.apple.com/publish and https://authors.apple.com/support/4574-publish-book-from-web (both read 3 October 2026)

Source: https://support.google.com/books/partner/table/6052428 (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## 8. UK tax basics (gov.uk only)

**8.1** "The trading allowance is a tax exemption of up to £1,000 a year for individuals with trading income."
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**8.2** You must register for Self Assessment if you have gross trading income over £1,000. For other income: over £1,000 up to £2,500, contact HMRC; over £2,500, register for Self Assessment.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**8.3** "You cannot claim expenses if you use your £1,000 tax-free 'trading allowance'."
Source: https://www.gov.uk/expenses-if-youre-self-employed (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**8.4** "You must tell HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) by 5 October 2026 if you need to complete a tax return for the previous tax year" (the 2025 to 2026 tax year). The general rule is 5 October after the end of the tax year.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/register-for-self-assessment (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**8.5** HMRC treats an author's profits, including copyright royalties, either as profits of a profession (trading income) or, where the writing does not amount to a profession, as miscellaneous income.
Sources: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim50701 and https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/business-income-manual/bim100205 (both read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**. These are HMRC internal manuals, not public guidance; the guide should say "it depends on your circumstances" and point to an accountant or HMRC.

## 9. UK copyright basics

**9.1** "You get copyright protection automatically - you do not have to apply or pay a fee." It covers original literary works. Marking with ©, your name and the year is optional.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/copyright (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**9.2** In the UK, copyright in written work lasts "70 years after the author's death".
Source: https://www.gov.uk/copyright/how-long-copyright-lasts (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

**9.3** UK law (section 9(3) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988) currently gives protection to wholly computer-generated works. In its Report on Copyright and Artificial Intelligence (published 18 March 2026 by DSIT, DCMS and the IPO), the government says "we propose that this specific type of protection should be removed, while copyright should continue to protect works created with AI assistance." This is a proposal, not yet a change in the law, as far as this page shows.
Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/report-and-impact-assessment-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence/report-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence (read 3 October 2026)
Status: **CONFIRMED**

## Use in the guide

Verify-box sentences for the writer. Each one keeps to what the official page said on 3 October 2026.

1. On KDP you choose a 35% or 70% eBook royalty; on amazon.co.uk the 70% option needs a list price between £1.77 and £12.99, and a delivery charge of £0.10 per MB is taken off each 70% sale. Check the KDP eBook Royalties and List Price Requirements help pages before you rely on this.

2. KDP pays paperback royalties on amazon.co.uk at 60% of the list price minus printing costs when you price at £7.99 or more, and at 50% when you price at £7.98 or less. Check the KDP Paperback Royalty help page before you rely on this.

3. A black-ink paperback on regular trim printed for amazon.co.uk costs £0.85 plus £0.01 per page once it passes the low page band (so about £2.85 for 200 pages), and KDP will not let you set a price that leaves your royalty below the printing cost. Check the KDP Printing Cost help page before you rely on this.

4. KDP pays roughly 60 days after the end of the month in which sales are reported, by direct deposit in pounds to a UK bank, with no minimum payment for direct deposit. Check the KDP "When will I get paid?" and Payment Options help pages before you rely on this.

5. As a UK individual you complete KDP's tax interview (Form W-8BEN); US withholding starts at 30% but may be cut to 0% under the UK and US tax treaty if you qualify and give your UK tax reference. Check the KDP Tax Withholding help page and the IRS treaty table before you rely on this.

6. If you used AI to write the text, make images or translate, KDP counts the book as AI-generated and you must say so when you publish; if you wrote it yourself and only used AI to edit or check it, that is AI-assisted and does not need declaring. Check the KDP Content Guidelines page before you rely on this.

7. KDP lets you add up to 7 keywords, pick 3 categories and write a description of up to 4,000 characters, and it currently limits you to creating 2 new titles per format each week. Check the KDP Keywords, Categories, Book Description and Create a Book help pages before you rely on this.

8. KDP's free ISBN works for paperbacks and hardcovers but can only be used on KDP; to publish the same edition elsewhere you need your own, which Nielsen sells in the UK at £93 for one or £174 for ten (VAT included). Check the KDP ISBN help page and the Nielsen UK ISBN Store before you rely on this.

9. If you publish a book in the UK, the British Library expects one free copy within a month of publication, and five other legal deposit libraries can ask for a copy within 12 months; self-published authors are included. Check the British Library legal deposit page before you rely on this.

10. Enrolling an eBook in KDP Select means it must be sold only as a Kindle eBook for each 90-day term (it renews automatically), in return for Kindle Unlimited page-read payments and promotions. Check the KDP Select help pages before you rely on this.

11. The first £1,000 of trading income each tax year is covered by the trading allowance; above that you must register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year ends, and you cannot use the allowance and claim expenses at the same time. Check GOV.UK's trading allowance and Self Assessment registration pages before you rely on this.

12. Your writing is protected by UK copyright automatically, with no form or fee, for 70 years after your death; the government has proposed removing the separate protection for works made wholly by computer, while keeping protection for work made with AI help. Check GOV.UK's copyright pages and the IPO's AI and copyright report before you rely on this.
