# KDP facts supplement, 6 October 2026

Only the facts below were rechecked. Other rules retain their 3 October verification date and recheck deadline.

- **11.1, confirmed:** all seven trims offered by Volume appear in the current paperback table. 5 x 8, 5.25 x 8, 5.5 x 8.5 and 6 x 9 allow 24 to 828 pages with black ink on white paper. 7 x 10 has the same range. 8.5 x 8.5 and 8.5 x 11 allow 24 to 590 pages. For black ink on cream paper, those first five trims allow 24 to 776 pages, and the two 8.5-inch trims allow 24 to 550 pages. Other ink/paper choices have different ranges. Large trims exceed 6.12 inches in width or 9 inches in height. [KDP trim table](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6).
- **11.2, confirmed:** inside minimums are 0.375 inches for 24 to 150 pages, 0.5 for 151 to 300, 0.625 for 301 to 500, 0.75 for 501 to 700 and 0.875 for 701 to 828. Outside minimums are 0.25 without bleed and 0.375 with bleed. [KDP margins](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6).
- **11.3, confirmed:** front/back text sits at least 0.125 inches inside trim; spine text has at least 0.0625 inches on either side. The page still gives conflicting wording at 79 pages, so Volume retains the safer 80-page threshold. [KDP cover guide](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020).
- **11.4, partial:** supplied barcodes have suggested dimensions of 2 x 1.2 inches and must sit at least 0.25 inches from spine and trim. KDP adds a barcode when none is supplied. The precise automatic location must be checked using the book's cover template and Print Previewer. [KDP barcodes](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G5HDYGP4BXLX4RUW).
