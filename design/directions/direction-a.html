The Calm Ward

De-escalation and Compassion in NHS Practice

By HMHarni Muharami

Summary

Every shift on a busy ward holds moments that could tip into conflict: a frightened relative, a patient in pain, a colleague at the end of a long night. From years on wards and in the emergency department, I show how to read distress early, talk people down with dignity, keep teams safe and learn from each incident without blame.

Target audience

Ward nurses, healthcare assistants and student nurses in NHS settings, plus the ward managers, matrons and clinical educators who shape safer teams.
