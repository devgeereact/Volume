The Calm Ward

De-escalation and Compassion in NHS Practice

Harni Muharami

Summary

Every shift on a busy ward holds moments that could tip into conflict: a frightened relative, a patient in pain, a colleague at the end of a long night. The Calm Ward is a practical and compassionate guide to meeting those moments well, written from years of ward and emergency department nursing.

I show how to read distress early, how to talk people down with dignity, and when to step back. Later chapters turn to the team itself: incivility between colleagues, psychological safety, honest reporting and the support staff need after an incident.

Who it is for

Ward nurses, healthcare assistants and student nurses working in NHS settings, and the ward managers, matrons and clinical educators who want calmer, safer teams.

Chapter summaries

1 The Reality Of Risk In Modern Healthcare

Opens on a crowded emergency department on a Friday night, then looks honestly at why aggression and incivility have become part of the job, what they cost staff and patients, and why calm is a skill that can be learnt.
