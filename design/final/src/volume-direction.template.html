The Calm Ward

De-escalation and Compassion in NHS Practice

Harni Muharami

Summary

Every shift on a busy ward holds moments that could tip into conflict: a frightened relative, a patient in pain, a colleague at the end of a long night. The Calm Ward is a practical guide to meeting those moments well, written from years of ward and emergency department nursing.

Chapter summaries

3Safe By Design: Environmental Factors

How sightlines, noise and waiting areas shape a ward's mood, and the small changes a team can make without a budget.

4The Art Of Verbal De-escalation

Opens with a scene at the nurses' station at three in the morning. Most incidents on a ward begin with an unmet need such as pain, fear or waiting. The chapter slows that moment down: what the body says before the words do, the talk down approach, and when stepping back is the safest choice.

5Addressing Horizontal Violence And Incivility

Incivility between colleagues, what it costs a team, and how to name it early.
